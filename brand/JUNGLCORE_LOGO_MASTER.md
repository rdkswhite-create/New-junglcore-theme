## Application rules
1. Use the **exact SVG logo artwork**, not typed “junglcore” in a substitute web font.
2. Keep letter proportions and spacing: preserve aspect ratio; never stretch, warp or independently auto-trace.
3. Do not add the ® symbol, botanical symbols, extra outlines, embossing or colour changes.
4. Black on light / white on dark only for the logo.
5. For future NUBE / ONE / OIL packaging print files, use the same black master artwork at each package's logo position; keep product names as separate uppercase typography. Confirm a vector print proof before production.
6. This document refers to *future packaging files*. Existing JPG product mockups have lettering baked into the photographs; updating the website logo does not automatically replace the artwork inside those images.
